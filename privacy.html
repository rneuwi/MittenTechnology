Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

The short version: we don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share your information. If you contact us, we use what you send only to reply and to work with you.

This policy explains how Mitten Technology LLC ("Mitten Technology," "we," "us") handles information when you visit this website or contact us through it. It applies only to this website, not to work we do for clients, which is covered by the agreement for that work.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you use our contact form or email us, we receive what you choose to send, typically:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your company name, if you include it
  • The contents of your message

Please don't send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information through the form.

Information collected automatically

We don't run analytics or advertising tools, and this site doesn't set its own cookies. However, some services that make the site work receive basic technical information from your browser:

  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses and request details for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Spam protection. When you start filling out the contact form, the page loads Google reCAPTCHA to block automated spam. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information (such as device and browser details) and may set cookies, and sends that data to Google for analysis. reCAPTCHA does not load unless you interact with the form. Its use is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Content delivery. The contact form's email software is loaded from the jsDelivr content network, which receives standard request information (like your IP address) when it delivers files.

Our fonts and images are hosted with the site itself, so loading the page does not contact Google Fonts or other font services.

How we use information

  • To read and reply to your message
  • To discuss, prepare, and carry out work you ask us to do
  • To keep the contact form free of spam and abuse
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We don't use your information for marketing lists, and we don't make automated decisions about you.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run the site and communicate with you: EmailJS, which delivers contact form submissions to our inbox (see the EmailJS Privacy Policy); our email provider; Google, for reCAPTCHA; and GitHub, for hosting. They may only use the information to provide their services.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, our clients, or the public, such as responding to a valid legal request.
  • In a business transfer, if Mitten Technology is merged or sold, in which case this policy continues to apply to information collected under it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the length of that relationship plus any period required for legal, tax, or accounting records. If we don't end up working together, we delete inquiries within two years, or sooner if you ask.

Security

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections, and we limit access to messages to the people who need it to respond. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you send us.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you and give you a copy
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information, unless we need to keep it for legal reasons

Depending on where you live (for example, California or other U.S. states with privacy laws, or the European Economic Area and United Kingdom), you may have additional rights under local law, including the right to object to or restrict certain uses and the right to complain to a data protection authority. Where those laws apply, we rely on your request (to reply to your message) and our legitimate interest in running and protecting this website as the basis for using your information.

To make a request, email hello@mittentechnology.com. We'll respond within 30 days, and we won't treat you differently for exercising your rights. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Because we don't track visitors or sell or share personal information, there is nothing for these signals to switch off on our end. We honor them by default.

Children

This site is meant for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, such as the policies of our service providers. We aren't responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll let you know before it takes effect.
